How to use
3 simple steps to get your walls boosted.
  1. Go to Boost tab and fill wall prices in millions + quantities.
  2. Enter your Supercell ID email, choose PayPal or Paysafecard and click PLACE ORDER.
  3. Check the Orders tab to see your order, open chat, in the end leave a Vouch.
